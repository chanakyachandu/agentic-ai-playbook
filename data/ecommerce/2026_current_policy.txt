SECTION 1: RETURNS, REFUNDS & REPLACEMENTS POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Standard 30-Day Return Window:
    Customers may return eligible items within 30 calendar days from the delivery date for a full refund back to the
    original payment method. Products must be in original condition, with original packaging, manuals, and accessories.

1.2 Electronics & High-Value Exceptions (14-Day Limit):
    Laptops, desktop computers, digital cameras, drones, and smartphones have a restricted 14-calendar-day return
    window from the delivery date. Items must show fewer than 10 cumulative hours of device runtime to qualify.

1.3 Restocking Fees:
    * Defective or Damaged Upon Arrival: $0 restocking fee. A prepaid return shipping label is provided immediately.
    * Opened, Non-Defective Items: A 15% restocking fee is deducted from the refund total to cover repackaging.
    * Unopened Items: $0 restocking fee if original manufacturer box seal is intact.

1.4 Non-Returnable & Final Sale Items:
    The following items cannot be returned or refunded under any circumstances:
    * Digital gift cards and prepaid store credit codes.
    * Downloadable software licenses and digital game keys once delivered via email.
    * Custom-configured or personalized engraved devices (e.g. customized laptop chassis).
    * In-ear headphones and earbuds once the sanitary plastic seal has been broken.

1.5 Refund Processing Timelines:
    Once the return package is received and inspected at our central fulfillment warehouse, refunds are initiated within
    48 business hours. Funds typically appear on the customer's credit card or bank account within 5 to 7 business days.


SECTION 2: SHIPPING RATES, DELIVERY TIERS & ORDER TRACKING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Domestic Delivery Options (Continental United States):
    * Standard Ground Shipping : $4.99 flat rate. FREE on all domestic orders of $50.00 or more. Delivers in 3 to 5
      business days via UPS or FedEx Ground.
    * Express 2-Day Air        : $14.99 flat rate. Guaranteed delivery within 2 business days.
    * Overnight Priority Rush  : $29.99 flat rate. Guaranteed next-business-day delivery. Orders must be submitted
      before 1:00 PM EST to ship the same day.

2.2 Signature Requirement for High-Value Orders:
    All shipments containing order totals of $500.00 or higher automatically require a direct adult signature (age 21+)
    upon delivery. Couriers are strictly prohibited from leaving packages unattended on porches or doorsteps.

2.3 International Shipping Coverage & Customs:
    NovaTech currently ships to 5 countries only: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, and Japan.
    * All international orders deliver within 7 to 12 business days via DHL Express.
    * International import taxes, customs duties, and local VAT are the sole responsibility of the customer and are
      collected by DHL prior to delivery. NovaTech does not calculate or refund foreign customs fees.


SECTION 3: WARRANTY COVERAGE & REPAIR SERVICES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Standard 1-Year Limited Hardware Warranty:
    Every new device purchased directly from NovaTech includes a complimentary 1-year limited hardware warranty
    commencing on the original invoice date.

3.2 What the Standard Warranty Covers:
    * Internal motherboard, CPU, and GPU hardware component failures.
    * Display screen defects with more than 3 dead or stuck pixels.
    * Internal battery capacity degradation below 80% of original rated capacity within the first 12 months.
    * AC power adapters and charging cables exhibiting manufacturing defects.

3.3 What the Standard Warranty Does NOT Cover:
    * Accidental drops, shattered screens, cracked chassis, or structural impact damage.
    * Liquid spills, water submersion, corrosion, or humidity damage.
    * Unauthorized third-party disassembly, unofficial software rooting, or aftermarket modifications.
    * Normal cosmetic wear, scratches, scuffs, and minor battery wear above 80% capacity.

3.4 NovaCare Extended Protection Plans:
    Customers can purchase optional 3-year NovaCare Protection within 30 days of hardware purchase:
    * Laptops & Desktops : $79.99 one-time fee.
    * Audio & Cameras    : $39.99 one-time fee.
    * Benefits: Covers up to 2 accidental damage replacements per year (drops or spills) with a fixed $50 deductible.

SECTION 5: CUSTOMER PRIVACY, SECURITY & DATA RETENTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Data Collection & Payment Security:
    NovaTech processes payments using PCI-DSS Level 1 tokenization. We never store complete credit card numbers
    or CVV security codes on our servers. Only the last 4 digits of the card and card brand are retained for receipts.

5.2 Inactive Data Retention Period:
    Customer transaction and invoice records are stored in secure, encrypted cloud cold-storage for exactly 7 years
    to comply with federal tax and auditing regulations, after which records are permanently purged.

5.3 Right to Deletion & Account Erasure (GDPR / CCPA):
    Customers may request complete erasure of their personal profile, marketing subscriptions, and browsing telemetry
    by emailing privacy@novatech-electronics.com or submitting a ticket at our customer portal. Erasure requests are
    processed within 30 calendar days.
