SECTION 1: HISTORICAL 2024 RETURNS & REFUNDS TERMS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1 Strict 14-Day Universal Return Window:
    Under 2024 terms, all purchases were subject to a strict 14-calendar-day return window from delivery date. No
    exceptions were granted for non-electronics or general accessories.

1.2 Mandatory 20% Restocking Fee:
    A mandatory 20% restocking fee was deducted from all opened, returned items regardless of condition, unless the
    unit was certified dead-on-arrival by an authorized technician within 48 hours of receipt.

1.3 Store Credit Only for Opened Goods:
    Refunds for opened electronics were issued exclusively as NovaTech Digital Store Credit vouchers valid for 180 days.
    Direct cash refunds were restricted to unopened merchandise with intact factory shrink-wrap.


SECTION 2: HISTORICAL 2024 SHIPPING & LOGISTICS GUIDELINES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 Domestic Shipping Tiers:
    * Standard Ground Delivery : $7.99 flat rate. Free only on orders of $75.00 or more.
    * 2-Day Air Courier        : $19.99 flat rate.
    * Overnight Rush           : $39.99 flat rate.

2.2 Geographic Coverage Restrictions:
    In 2024, NovaTech shipped exclusively to the continental United States and Canada. No European or Asian shipping
    destinations were supported under logistics agreement LOG-2024-A.


SECTION 3: HISTORICAL 2024 WARRANTY LIMITATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 Limited 90-Day Parts & Labor Warranty:
    The 2024 base warranty covered hardware defects for exactly 90 calendar days from invoice date. Factory-refurbished
    merchandise included a 30-day limited guarantee.

3.2 Battery & Screen Exclusions:
    Batteries were considered consumable components and were explicitly excluded from warranty coverage after 30 days.
    Display screens were only eligible for RMA replacement if showing 5 or more clustered dead pixels.
