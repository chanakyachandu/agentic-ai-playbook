Q01. What happens if my package is lost or stolen before delivery?
     Answer: If carrier tracking indicates "Delivered" but you have not received your package, you must file a lost
     package trace within 5 business days by emailing support@novatech-electronics.com. NovaTech coordinates directly
     with UPS/FedEx to conduct a GPS scan investigation. If unresolved after 72 hours, a free replacement is shipped.

Q02. Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?
     Answer: Address modifications are permitted only within 45 minutes of order placement. Once warehouse
     status transitions to "Pick & Pack", the address is locked and reroutes must be handled with the carrier.

Q03. What is the price match guarantee policy?
     Answer: NovaTech price-matches major certified retailers (Amazon Direct, Best Buy, B&H Photo) within 14 calendar
     days of purchase. The competitor item must be identical, brand new in-box, and currently in-stock. Third-party
     marketplace sellers and auction listings are strictly excluded from price matching.

Q04. Does NovaTech offer student, military, or educator discounts?
     Answer: Yes. Verified active students, educators, and military personnel receive an ongoing 10% discount on
     eligible computing accessories and peripherals via our ID.me portal integration. Core laptop chassis and GPUs
     are capped at a 5% maximum promotional discount.

Q05. How do I return a bundle deal if I only want to keep one item?
     Answer: Bundle purchases (e.g., Camera + Lens Kit) must be returned as a complete package to receive a full refund.
     If a customer retains individual components from a bundle, the retained item is billed at its full standalone
     MSRP price, and only the remaining net difference is refunded.

Q06. What should I do if my screen arrives with dead pixels?
     Answer: Displays with 3 or fewer isolated dead pixels conform to standard ISO-9241-307 Class II manufacturing
     tolerances and are not classified as defective under basic warranty. Displays with 4 or more dead pixels, or any
     cluster of 2 adjacent dead pixels, qualify for immediate complimentary RMA advance exchange within 30 days.

Q07. Can I cancel a pre-order before it ships?
     Answer: Pre-orders can be cancelled at any time prior to warehouse allocation for a 100% full refund with zero
     cancellation penalty. Once credit cards are charged upon stock arrival, standard return procedures apply.

Q08. What are the rules regarding refurbished and open-box items?
     Answer: Certified Refurbished products include a comprehensive 6-month limited warranty and a 14-day return window.
     Open-Box items sold in Grade-A condition include full remaining manufacturer warranty periods.

Q09. How does international customs duty collection work for UK and European customers?
     Answer: For shipments to the UK and Germany, orders are shipped Delivered At Place (DAP). Local customs offices
     generate import VAT invoices directly through DHL Express. If a customer refuses customs duty payment, the package
     is returned to our USA warehouse, and return logistics expenses ($35 flat rate) are deducted from the refund.

Q10. Can I return a laptop if I already installed Linux or upgraded the internal RAM?
     Answer: User hardware modifications (opening chassis or swapping SSD/RAM) do not void warranty unless damage
     occurs during modification. However, for 14-day retail returns, the original factory components, original operating
     system, and recovery partitions must be fully restored prior to warehouse return inspection.

Q11. What is the policy for hazardous materials, lithium batteries, and drone air transit?
     Answer: Devices containing standalone uninstalled lithium-ion batteries cannot ship via Next-Day Air due to FAA and
     IATA cargo regulations. Such orders default automatically to Standard Ground transport.

Q12. What happens if an item is damaged in transit by the carrier?
     Answer: Customers must take clear photos of the damaged shipping box and report the issue within 48 hours
     of delivery. A prepaid return label is generated, and a prioritized replacement order is dispatched immediately.

Q13. How do gift receipts and holiday return extensions operate?
     Answer: Purchases made between November 1 and December 24 qualify for our Extended Holiday Return Policy, allowing
     returns through January 31 of the subsequent year. Returns accompanied by a gift receipt receive store credit only.

Q14. How do I verify my device runtime hours for a camera or laptop return?
     Answer: Our warehouse diagnostic scanners inspect internal NVMe S.M.A.R.T. controller power-on logs and camera
     electronic shutter actuation counts. Devices recording over 10 cumulative operating hours are classified as used
     and assessed the mandatory 15% open-box restocking fee.

Q15. Can corporate business accounts receive Net-30 invoice terms?
     Answer: Corporate accounts spending over $25,000 annually can apply for Net-30 credit lines upon credit check
     approval by our corporate underwriting team (b2b-finance@novatech-electronics.com).

Q16. What is the procedure if a customer reports unauthorized fraudulent purchases?
     Answer: When fraud is flagged, our Security Operations Center immediately locks the customer profile, cancels
     unfulfilled transit manifests with carrier intercept APIs, and routes the transaction to our fraud auditing queue.

Q17. Are environmental electronic disposal and recycling fees refundable?
     Answer: State electronic waste recycling fees (such as California eWaste fees) are non-refundable once the physical
     item has been delivered, in accordance with state environmental compliance statutes.

Q18. What happens if I return an item without all original box cables and adapters?
     Answer: Missing accessories incur standard replacement deductions: $15 for missing HDMI/USB-C cables, and $45 for
     missing proprietary high-wattage AC charging bricks.

Q19. How do I upgrade from Silver Tier to Gold Tier in NovaClub?
     Answer: Tier status upgrades automatically within 24 hours of total qualifying annual spend reaching $1,000.00.
     Qualifying spend excludes shipping fees, sales taxes, and returned merchandise totals.

Q20. Can loyalty points be redeemed on promotional or Black Friday doorbuster items?
     Answer: Yes. NovaClub points convert into cash voucher codes that function as standard payment tenders, valid on
     all catalog items including clearance and doorbuster promotions.

Q21. How does the NovaCare accidental replacement claim work?
     Answer: Customers initiate claims via the online portal, submit photo proof of the cracked screen or water spill,
     pay the $50 deductible fee, and receive an advance replacement unit with a prepaid box to return the damaged item.

Q22. What is the maximum number of items allowed per order for high-demand GPU releases?
     Answer: To combat scalping bots, new-generation graphics cards and limited consoles are restricted to
     a strict limit of 1 unit per customer and household address. Duplicate orders are automatically purged.

Q23. Does NovaTech provide trade-in credits for older electronics?
     Answer: Yes. Customers can input older device serial numbers into our Trade-In calculator to receive instant
     estimated quotes. Once the device arrives at our inspection depot, digital store credit is credited within 3 days.

Q24. What are the customer support phone operating hours?
     Answer: Toll-free phone support (+1-800-555-0199) operates Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST,
     and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST. Platinum VIP members receive dedicated 24/7/365 priority routing.

Q25. How do I initiate a formal legal dispute or binding arbitration request?
     Answer: Formal legal correspondence must be served via certified mail to NovaTech Legal Operations, 500 Technology
     Way, Suite 400, Austin, TX 78701, in accordance with Section 14 of our Terms of Service.
